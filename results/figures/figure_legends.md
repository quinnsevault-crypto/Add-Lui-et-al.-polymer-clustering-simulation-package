# Figure legends

**Figure 1. MM32 polymer comparison over simulation time.** Simulated MM32 luminescence under no-polymer control conditions and with P1, P2, or P3 at 7,000 a.u. The upper panel shows the luminescence ratio relative to the same-seed, same-time no-polymer control; the dashed line marks a ratio of 1. The lower panel shows log10 mean population luminescence. Lines show the mean across 10 independent seeds, and shaded bands show ±1 standard deviation. Simulations span 0–30,000 a.u. and are sampled every 1,000 a.u. The solver uses an operator-split tau-leap with a 100-a.u. step, so results are approximate rather than exact direct-Gillespie trajectories.

**Figure 2. BB170 polymer comparison over simulation time.** Simulated BB170 luminescence under no-polymer control conditions and with P1, P2, or P3 at 7,000 a.u. The upper panel shows the luminescence ratio relative to the same-seed, same-time no-polymer control; the dashed line marks a ratio of 1. The lower panel shows log10 mean population luminescence. Lines show the mean across 10 independent seeds, and shaded bands show ±1 standard deviation. Simulations span 0–30,000 a.u. and are sampled every 1,000 a.u. The solver uses an operator-split tau-leap with a 100-a.u. step, so results are approximate rather than exact direct-Gillespie trajectories.

**Figure 3. Endpoint fold-change comparison across strains and polymer treatments.** Mean simulated luminescence fold change at 30,000 a.u. for MM32 and BB170 with P1, P2, or P3 at 7,000 a.u., relative to the same-strain, same-seed no-polymer control. Points show the mean across 10 independent seeds; horizontal error bars show ±1 standard deviation. The dashed vertical line at 1 indicates no change relative to the paired control. Results are from the 100-a.u. operator-split tau-leap approximation.

**Interpretation note.** Fold change here means a ratio to the paired simulated no-polymer control, not experimental luminescence normalized by optical density. These figures cover one nonzero polymer concentration and are exploratory; they do not represent the complete concentration series or affinity grid from the paper.
